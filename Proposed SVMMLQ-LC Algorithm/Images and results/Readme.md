# Experimental Figures for the SVMMLQ-LC Manuscript

This folder contains the final publication figures used in the revised manuscript:

**SVMMLQ-LC: An Intelligent Machine Learning-Based Load-Balancing Framework with Multi-Level Queue Scheduling and Least-Connection Selection for Software-Defined Networking**

The figures correspond to the updated experimental setup, proposed methodology, SDN topology, traffic processing workflow, queue-scheduling mechanism, clustering analysis, and performance evaluation.

## Figure List

- **Figure 1:** SDN topology and deployment of the SVMMLQ-LC controller.
- **Figure 2:** Overall workflow of the proposed SVMMLQ-LC framework.
- **Figure 3:** Single-switch Mininet topology for SDN traffic generation and packet capture.
- **Figure 4:** Pearson correlation heatmap of numerical flow-level features.
- **Figure 5:** Elbow method for selecting the number of K-Means clusters.
- **Figure 6:** K-Means clustering results for the selected traffic-flow groups.
- **Figure 7:** Threshold-based multi-level queue scheduling mechanism with starvation control.
- **Figure 8:** Comparison of average response time among the evaluated load-balancing methods.
- **Figure 9:** Comparison of average processing time among the evaluated load-balancing methods.
- **Figure 10:** Comparison of the degree of load balancing among the evaluated methods.
- **Figure 11:** Sensitivity of Class-1 P99 waiting time to the threshold value.
- **Figure 12:** Sensitivity of Class-0 mean waiting time to the threshold value.
- **Figure 13:** Sensitivity of Jain’s fairness index to the threshold value.

## Reproducibility

The figures are based on the experimental SDN environment, traffic dataset, preprocessing procedures, K-Means clustering, SVM classification, multi-level queue scheduling, and Least-Connection server-selection process described in the manuscript.

Supporting datasets, source code, and experimental materials are available in the corresponding folders of this repository.
